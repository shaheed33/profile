Economist working on public debt, climate risk and energy markets.

I'm Shaheed, from Maldives. I'm a PhD candidate in Economics and Econometrics at the University of York, where my thesis looks at debt sustainability and at how climate change and climate-related disasters affect what governments pay to borrow. The final chapter turns to Australia's National Electricity Market.

Before York I worked in public finance in Maldives for more than a decade, from price statistics and tax audits to running the finances of state-owned companies. I hold Australian permanent residency and am moving to Sydney.

Projects

Maldives National Debt Clock

A live public tracker of what the Maldivian government owes, what it pays in interest and where its revenue comes from. Built from official MMA, Ministry of Finance and MIRA data and written for people without a finance background.

Visit mvdebtclock.org

Could the NEM run without coal?

An interactive model of wind, solar and storage across Australia's National Electricity Market. Change the generation mix and battery size and see whether demand is still met, half hour by half hour.

See the October 2024 replication

When is electricity cheap?

Wholesale prices in the NEM by hour of day, season, year and region, with simple, demand-weighted and median measures.
