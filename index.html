About me

I'm Shaheed, from Maldives, completing a PhD in Economics at the University of York on debt sustainability, climate risk and sovereign bond yields, and the Australian economy, with a focus on regional development and the National Electricity Market.

I work with Stata, Python, R and Excel, and have foundational training in PLEXOS energy modelling. I've worked across economic development, statistics, tax and public finance in Maldives, in government ministries, state owned enterprises and a local authority company.

I'm most interested in analytics, policy and research roles in economic development and energy, and especially where the two meet.
