Side projects

NEM storage simulator

Choose a future grid of wind, solar and storage, then test it against five years of real weather from the NEM, every half hour. Started as a replication of Andrew Grogan's spreadsheet, shared in his LinkedIn post.

NEM price patterns

Five years of wholesale electricity prices by hour, season and region. See when power is cheap, how often prices go below zero, and how major events moved the market.

MSc dissertation: bubbles in energy and food commodity prices

MSc Economics and Econometrics, University of Nottingham, 2021. Awarded a Distinction.

Tests 12 energy and agricultural commodity prices from 1970 to 2020 for explosive price episodes, dates when each began and ended, and checks whether they spread from one market to another, using right tailed unit root tests and a wild bootstrap test for co explosive behaviour.
