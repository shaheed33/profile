About this site

The Maldives National Debt Clock shows how much the Maldivian government owes, what that means for each citizen, and how the government's income and spending compare. It is built entirely from official statistics published by the Maldives Monetary Authority, and every figure is explained on the methodology page.

Public debt affects everyone. It shapes what the government can spend on schools, hospitals and islands, and what future generations will have to repay. The aim is to make those numbers easy to see and understand, without taking a political side.

The site is independent and is not affiliated with the Maldives Monetary Authority, the Ministry of Finance or any political party. Its code is open, so anyone can check how it works: github.com/shaheed33/maldives-debt-clock.

Who made it

Shaheed is a Maldivian economist and PhD candidate in Economics and Econometrics at the University of York, where his research looks at how climate vulnerability affects the cost of government borrowing. He has worked in public finance in the Maldives, including as a financial controller in a state-owned enterprise.
