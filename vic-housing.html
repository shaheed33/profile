Is Victoria building enough homes?

In 2023 the Victorian Government set a goal of 800,000 new homes in the ten years from 2024. This page checks, every month, how many are actually being finished, and whether each council is approving enough to meet its share of the state's longer plan to 2051.

How to read this

What counts as a home built

Homes finished are the dwellings the Australian Bureau of Statistics counts as completed each quarter in its Building Activity survey, covering houses, townhouses and apartments, built privately or by government. The ten years are counted from January 2024 to December 2033. The government reports its own progress in its own way, which may differ.

Why councils are judged on approvals

Completions are not published by council, so the council view uses building approvals, the permits issued before building starts. Statewide, the homes finished in a year have been close to the number approved about a year earlier, so approvals are a fair early read. Each council's target to 2051 is spread evenly over the 28 years from 2024, and its approvals over the latest twelve months are compared with that yearly figure.

This yardstick is this page's own, not the government's. Plan for Victoria asks councils to make enough room in their planning rules for their target, and councils have argued the targets should be judged as capacity rather than homes completed. Approvals also depend on builders, buyers, interest rates and construction costs, not only on councils.

Things to keep in mind

Approvals bounce around from month to month, especially where a single apartment tower is approved, so a small council can look far ahead or far behind in one year. Mitchell's target is split, with homes inside Melbourne's growth boundary and in the regional part of the shire. Growth area councils such as Melton, Wyndham and Casey have targets that are mostly new estates on the urban fringe.

Sources

Australian Bureau of Statistics Data API for Building Activity (dwellings started, finished and under construction), Building Approvals for Victoria and for each local government area, and Victoria's estimated resident population. Council housing targets are the final 2051 targets published by the Victorian Government with Plan for Victoria in February 2025. Council boundaries are the ABS 2025 local government areas. The page refreshes each month after the ABS release.
