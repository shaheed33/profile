How the figures are worked out

  • New means a wind, solar, battery or pumped hydro unit that was not operating in AEMO's January 2021 release, the first after the Roadmap became law. Operating includes units being commissioned.
  • Committed projects have finance and approvals. Anticipated ones are well advanced. Proposed ones are announced only, and many will not be built.
  • The official objectives count generation by annual output and storage by duration, so this page measures capacity as a guide. The storage bar includes batteries of any duration.
  • The Year column shows when an operating project first appeared as operating in an AEMO release. For other projects it shows the full commercial use year the developer reported, in italics. Many proposed projects have not reported one.
  • AEMO's NSW region includes the ACT, so ACT projects such as the Capital Battery are counted here.
  • The page checks AEMO every week and rebuilds itself when a new release appears.
  • Sources AEMO Generation Information, NSW Net Zero Commission 2026 Annual Progress Report and NSW Electricity Infrastructure Roadmap.
