        How the figures are worked out

        • New means a wind, solar, battery or pumped hydro unit that was not operating in AEMO's January 2021 release, the first after the Roadmap became law. Operating includes units being commissioned.
        • Committed projects have finance and approvals. Anticipated ones are well advanced. Proposed ones are announced only, and many will not be built.
        • Targets. The Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act sets minimums of 12 GW of new renewable generation and 2 GW of long-duration storage by 2030, and 28 GWh of long-duration storage by 2034. In its 2025 Infrastructure Investment Objectives Report, the NSW Consumer Trustee (ASL, formerly AEMO Services) raised the targets to 16 GW and 42 GWh, warning that the minimums alone would push prices up as coal closes.
        • Long-duration storage is storage that can run at full power for at least 8 hours. The page works out each unit's hours from the capacity and energy AEMO reports. Snowy 2.0 is shown separately because it was already committed when the Roadmap began.
        • The official objectives count generation by annual output, so this page measures capacity as a guide.
        • The Year column shows when an operating project first appeared as operating in an AEMO release. For other projects it shows the full commercial use year the developer reported, in italics. Many proposed projects have not reported one.
        • AEMO's file has no coordinates, so map locations come from the Open Electricity station register and are matched by AEMO unit code or site name. Some projects borrow the location of a nearby station of the same name and are marked approximate.
        • AEMO's NSW region includes the ACT, so ACT projects such as the Capital Battery are counted here.
        • The page checks AEMO every week and rebuilds itself when a new release appears.
        • Sources AEMO Generation Information, NSW Net Zero Commission 2026 Annual Progress Report, NSW Electricity Infrastructure Roadmap, ASL's 2025 Infrastructure Investment Objectives Report and Open Electricity for locations.
