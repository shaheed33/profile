Is NSW on track for 2030?

The NSW Electricity Infrastructure Roadmap aims for at least 12 GW of new renewable generation and 2 GW of long-duration storage by 2030. This page measures progress using AEMO's register of every generation and storage project in the state.

How the figures are worked out

  • Data comes from AEMO's NEM Generation Information, published each quarter, using one release about every six months since January 2021. Only projects in the NSW region of the market are counted, which includes Snowy Hydro.
  • New means any wind, solar, battery or pumped hydro unit that was not already operating in AEMO's January 2021 release, the first after the Electricity Infrastructure Investment Act was passed. Units are matched across releases by their AEMO unit code, or by site name where there is none. Operating includes units still being commissioned.
  • Committed projects have finance and approvals in place. Anticipated projects are well advanced but not yet committed. Proposed projects are publicly announced only, and many will not be built.
  • The official objectives count generation by expected annual output and storage by how many hours it can run, so this page is a guide to progress in capacity terms rather than the official measure. The storage figure includes batteries of any duration, while the Roadmap target is for long-duration storage of eight hours or more.
  • The 16 GW line is the more ambitious generation goal set by the Consumer Trustee in 2025.
