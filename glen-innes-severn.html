REPRODUCIBILITY & BOUNDARIES

Sources, definitions and limitations

Official numbers are dated explicitly. These analyses combine the ABS 2021 Census, the website's local-economy datasets, and the council's publicly stated strategy and role requirements. Announced investments are not realised investment; visitors are not unique visitors; employment by residence is not employment by workplace; zoned land is not necessarily investment-ready. Proposed measures require fresh evidence and Council-held information.
